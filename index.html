Yasmine,we see you.

I want you to know that your efforts are seen and appreciated.

Being a woman who shouldn’t have had to provide or work anyway,

you have been not just the greatest mother, but also the best role model we could have ever asked for.

Since we came into this world, we saw you working non-stop in school;

you didn’t care that you didn’t have a car and always found a way to show up.

You were the best. You were always the best at everything you put your mind to.

You are a sign of discipline, and we are so lucky to have you and so proud of you.

We don’t show our appreciation often, so we thought we should tell you this.

We love you, Mom.Thank you for everything.
